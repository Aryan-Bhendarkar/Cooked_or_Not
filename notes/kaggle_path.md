# Path to Kaggle Competitions Expert / Master

## Requirements (Competitions track) — verify on kaggle.com/progression
- **Expert:** 2 bronze medals in competitions
- **Master:** 1 gold + 2 silver medals
- **Grandmaster:** 5 gold medals, at least 1 solo
- Medal cutoffs depend on number of teams. Small (under 100 teams): top 40% bronze / top 20% silver / top 10% gold. Very large (1000+ teams): top 10% bronze / top 5% silver / top 10 places gold. Mid-size competitions use in-between rules — read each competition's page.
- **No medals** from InClass, Playground and Getting Started competitions. => THIS college competition gives skills and marks, NOT Kaggle medals. Medals come from Featured / Research / Analytics competitions.
- Other Kaggle tracks (Notebooks, Datasets, Discussion) have their own, easier Expert tiers; check their requirements if "Expert" in any track is acceptable.

## Honest timeline (my estimate, not a Kaggle statement)
- Expert: 2 bronze = realistically 2-4 serious competitions entered from the first week and worked on consistently. Months, not weeks.
- Master: much harder (1 gold + 2 silver). Typically needs teaming with stronger people and a year or more of steady work.
- Fast = focus + early start + learning from top solutions + good teammates. NOT copying public notebooks (they score about the public average, not medal level).

## Skill ladder (build in this order)
1. Validation and leakage (this competition drills it: group folds, shift, shuffle test)
2. Tabular: LightGBM/XGBoost/CatBoost + feature engineering + target-encoding pitfalls (most Featured medals)
3. Computer vision: pretrained backbones, augmentation, fine-tuning, TTA (this competition)
4. Ensembling / stacking, rank averaging, hill-climbing weights on OOF
5. Experiment hygiene and compute (Kaggle GPUs, saving OOF/test preds, notebooks vs scripts)
6. NLP / LLM fine-tuning (add later)

## How to pick the next (medal) competition
- Featured or Research, awards medals, at least 5-6 weeks left, evaluation metric I understand, data size that fits free Kaggle compute.
- Prefer tabular or vision (my current skills). Enter in week 1, not week 5.
- Read the rules about external data, pretrained models and AI-assisted coding before starting.
- One competition at a time; join a team once I have a validated baseline to contribute.

## Learn from finished competitions (free practice)
- Pick 3 past Featured competitions (2 tabular, 1 vision). For each: read the top-5 write-ups, rebuild the core idea from scratch, make a late submission to see where it would have ranked.
- Keep one page per competition: what the winners did that I didn't think of.
